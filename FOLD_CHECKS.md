# Still to see on the Fold

Things the emulator can't show, checked by hand on the Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra. Officina reads this list; tick an item there, or here.

## Readings

- [ ] With Fold Taskbar updated (Spia build) and on, the Readings card disappears within a few minutes
- [ ] Samsung reports the charge counter (µAh): the rates move in fractions, not whole 1% steps
- [ ] After a day, the main and cover rates separate clearly (main several times the cover)
- [ ] Turning or half-folding the phone doesn't log a screen change

## Gauge

- [ ] Cover upright: the tank, both figures and the lamp in view
- [ ] Cover turned, main screen both ways: the gauge in its own pane, fitted to the height
- [ ] Today's line colours match the screens used

## Widget

- [ ] Adds from Smart Launcher's widget list, and draws at its own size
- [ ] Turning the phone shows the picture made for that shape
- [ ] Updates within five minutes of use

## Reserve lamp

- [ ] Lights with the chosen hours, with a quiet notification
- [ ] Spia shows the notification as a champagne lamp
- [ ] Goes out on the charger, and the notification is withdrawn
